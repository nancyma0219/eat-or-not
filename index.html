📏 How to read the numbers
Violation points (hygiene)
The city inspector adds points for each problem found, more for serious ones. Lower is better: A = 0–13, B = 14–27, C = 28+. "Critical" violations are the ones most likely to make someone sick.
⚠️ Grade may be outdated
A restaurant that scores 14+ on a first inspection isn't graded on the spot; it gets re-inspected weeks later. Until then the old grade stays in the window, so Meowchi flags it.
Rat-risk points
Meowchi's own score, lower is better: 0–2 low, 3–5 moderate, 6+ high. Points come from pest violations inside the restaurant (3 years) and city rodent inspections that found rats at other buildings within 150 m (1 year). Each card shows exactly which points came from where.
Google rating and "adj"
The adjusted rating pulls places with few reviews toward a typical 4.2, so a 5.0 from 6 reviews (adj 4.29) can't beat a 4.7 from 2,000 (adj 4.69).
Verdicts
GO: clean and well-loved · GO IF SPLURGING: same, but $$ or more · TASTY BUT CHECK: loved, but some inspection marks · FINE: nothing special either way · SKIP: grade C, closed by the city in the last year, or weak reviews · NOT ENOUGH DATA: rating or grade missing. Hygiene is never averaged with taste: a dirty kitchen is a SKIP however good the reviews.
Ranking (comparisons)
Sorted by verdict first (GO → GO IF SPLURGING → TASTY BUT CHECK → FINE → NOT ENOUGH DATA → SKIP), then by the review-adjusted rating, then by fewer violation points. The order is computed, not picked by taste.
